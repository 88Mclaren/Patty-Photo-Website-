A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Patty.

Welcome to Patty Arana Photo! I'm passionate about capturing the moments that make life's celebrations meaningful. From the excitement of a wedding day to the joy of gathering with family and friends, every event has a story worth preserving.

My goal is to create photographs that bring you back to the feelings, people, and little details that made your day special. I believe the most memorable images come from genuine moments, natural interactions, and the connections we share with one another.

Whether you're planning an intimate celebration or a lively event, I'd love to help you preserve memories you'll be able to look back on for years to come.
